### Data‑Driven Global Market Insights for Business Decision Making

## 📌 Overview
This project is a production‑grade economic analytics dashboard designed to help
businesses, investors, and consultants make smarter international decisions using
data‑driven insights.

Instead of presenting raw economic indicators, this dashboard transforms
multiple global datasets into a unified **Opportunity Scoring System** and
automated business insights.

The goal is simple: **turn public economic data into actionable intelligence.**

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
Organizations expanding internationally face key challenges:
- Fragmented and inconsistent economic data
- Difficulty comparing countries objectively
- Limited frameworks for risk‑reward evaluation

This project addresses these problems by combining economic, labor, cost,
governance, and tourism indicators into a single analytical system.

---

## 👥 Target Users
- Startups planning international expansion
- Investors screening global markets
- Management consultants & analysts
- Policy and economic research professionals

---

## 📊 Data Sources
The dashboard integrates five real‑world datasets:
- GDP per capita (market wealth)
- Unemployment rate (labor market health)
- Cost of living & purchasing power
- Corruption index (governance risk)
- Tourism flows & GDP dependency

All datasets are country‑level and merged into a unified analytical model.

---

## 🧠 Key Features
✅ Unified country‑level economic dataset  
✅ Custom feature engineering (affordability, labor stress, dependency risk)  
✅ AI‑style Opportunity Score (0–100)  
✅ Market segmentation using clustering  
✅ Automated business insight generation  
✅ Interactive data visualizations  

---

## 🚀 Opportunity Score (Core Intelligence)
Each country is assigned an Opportunity Score using weighted indicators:

- Positive signals:
  - GDP per capita
  - Purchasing power
  - Tourism receipts

- Risk signals:
  - Unemployment rate
  - Corruption index

The result is a clear, explainable score that supports real business decisions.

---

## 📈 Market Segmentation
Countries are grouped into market types such as:
- Stable high‑income markets
- High‑risk / high‑reward economies
- Emerging affordable markets
- Tourism‑dependent economies

This enables faster strategic filtering.

---

## 🤖 Automated Insights
The dashboard generates plain‑English insights such as:
> “Attractive market but requires strong compliance controls.”

This makes the analysis usable for both technical and non‑technical stakeholders.
